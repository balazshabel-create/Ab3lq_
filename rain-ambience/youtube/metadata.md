## Growth notes

- **Longer versions:** sleep viewers prefer 3–10 hour versions, and these get far more watch time per viewer. The generator renders any length (`--minutes 180`, `--minutes 600`), and the visual loop simply repeats.
- **Series:** release a series (window rain, thunderstorm, forest rain, tent rain, rain with a fireplace) and link everything to one playlist.
- **Upload time:** upload in the evening of your main audience's time zone (around 19:00–21:00). That's when sleep searches peak.
